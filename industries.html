Sectors we operate in

Each sector has unique facility management needs. We tailor our services to match the specific requirements of your property type.

Residential Complexes

Gated communities, housing societies and apartment complexes require consistent security, cleanliness and maintenance.

  • 24/7 security and gate management
  • Common area housekeeping
  • Waste collection and OWC operations
  • Gardening and grounds maintenance

IT Parks & Business Centres

Corporate campuses and tech parks demand professional presentation, reliable security and uninterrupted facility support.

  • Access control and visitor management
  • Daily office and common area cleaning
  • Electrical and plumbing upkeep
  • On-site facility manager

Malls & Retail Outlets

High-footfall retail environments need visible security, spotless presentation and rapid response to incidents.

  • Trained security and bouncer staff
  • Continuous housekeeping during trading hours
  • Crowd management and access control
  • Emergency response readiness

Hospitals & Healthcare

Healthcare facilities require infection-controlled cleaning, discreet security and strict compliance with hygiene standards.

  • Infection-control housekeeping protocols
  • Biomedical waste handling support
  • Discreet, trained security personnel
  • 24/7 availability for critical facilities

Educational Institutions

Schools, colleges and universities need safe campuses, clean facilities and reliable staff who understand the environment.

  • Campus security and gate management
  • Classroom and common area cleaning
  • Grounds and garden maintenance
  • Police-verified, background-checked staff

Hotels & Resorts

Hospitality properties demand impeccable presentation, attentive security and seamless behind-the-scenes facility support.

  • Front-of-house and back-of-house cleaning
  • Uniformed, groomed security staff
  • Landscaping and pool area upkeep
  • Event security and crowd management

Industrial & Warehouses

Manufacturing plants and logistics facilities need robust security, industrial cleaning and strict safety compliance.

  • Perimeter and asset security
  • Industrial housekeeping and waste management
  • Electrical and plumbing maintenance
  • Fire safety trained personnel

Banks & Financial Institutions

Banks and financial offices require discreet, professional security and consistently clean, presentable premises.

  • Armed and unarmed security options
  • Daily office and ATM area cleaning
  • Visitor access management
  • Verified, background-checked personnel

Events & Public Gatherings

Events require experienced crowd management, visible security presence and rapid response capability throughout.

  • Trained bouncers and event security
  • Entry management and frisking
  • QRT on standby for emergencies
  • Short-term and long-term deployments
