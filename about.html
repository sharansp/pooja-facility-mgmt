Our story

From a single security post to 2,135+ professionals

Pooja Facility Management Services Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted facility management company specialising in security services, housekeeping and manpower solutions. With a workforce of 2,135+ trained professionals, we deliver customised and dependable services that enable our clients to maintain safe, clean and well-managed premises.

Founded in 2008 in Pune, the company has grown steadily by prioritising discipline, reliability and genuine care for both clients and staff. Today we serve 100+ organisations across residential, corporate, industrial and institutional sectors throughout Maharashtra.

"Our Managing Director began his career as a security guard, gaining 8 years of hands-on field experience, and today brings over 35 years of experience in the security industry."

— Leadership philosophy, Pooja Facility Management Services
