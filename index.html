About us

Built on discipline. Run with integrity.

Pooja Facility Management Services Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted facility management company specializing in security services, housekeeping, and manpower solutions. With a workforce of 2,135+ trained professionals, we deliver customized and dependable services that enable our clients to maintain safe, clean, and well-managed premises.

What truly sets us apart is the leadership behind the organization — practically driven, disciplined, and people-focused, from the ground up.

Housekeeping

Clean, safe and well-maintained — every single day

Our housekeeping teams follow structured daily, weekly and monthly cleaning cycles, using colour-coded equipment to prevent cross-contamination and eco-friendly products wherever possible.

  • Daily, weekly & deep cleaning cycles
  • High-rise facade, lift & stairwell cleaning
  • Waste segregation & OWC operations
  • Trained, uniformed & hygiene-certified staff
